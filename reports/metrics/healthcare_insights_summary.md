# MediAssist AI - Healthcare Insights Report Summary

## Overview
This document summarizes the insights and statistics extracted from the reports located in `data/raw/reports/`.

## 1. India National Fact Sheet
- **Purpose**: Provides a comprehensive overview of the healthcare infrastructure and health outcomes in India.
- **Key Metrics**:
  - Healthcare facility density by state.
  - Prevalence of common non-communicable diseases.
  - Rural vs. Urban healthcare accessibility gaps.
  - National health insurance coverage statistics.

## 2. Final Report
- **Purpose**: Project-specific final report detailing the system's objectives and methodology.
- **Insights**:
  - Implementation of TF-IDF + Logistic Regression for baseline symptom classification.
  - Integration of DistilBERT for conversational understanding.
  - Use of Qwen2.5 for high-level medical reasoning and uncertainty estimation.
  - Importance of modular preprocessing for medical data integrity.

## Conclusion
These reports highlight the need for a hybrid AI system (MediAssist) that can bridge the gap between rural healthcare facilities and specialized medical advice. The data extracted from these reports has been used to inform the hospital recommendation engine.
